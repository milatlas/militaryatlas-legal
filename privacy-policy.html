1. Introduction

Military Atlas ("we," "our," or "the app") is a military news and defense intelligence application. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use our app, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

By using Military Atlas, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifying information.

Your watchlist (countries and equipment you save) is stored anonymously on our servers, linked only to a randomly generated device identifier — never to your identity.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

Device Advertising Identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID)
Where permitted (see Section 5 — Advertising), your device's advertising identifier is read by Google AdMob and shared with Google and its advertising partners so that ads can be personalized and measured. On iOS this requires your permission; in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland it requires your consent; on Android elsewhere you can opt out in your device settings. Otherwise, ads are shown without personalization. This identifier is a resettable, non-permanent ID assigned by your operating system. Pro and Founder users are not shown ads and are not asked for this permission.

IP Address and Approximate Location
Collected automatically by our cloud infrastructure (Firebase/Google) and by Google AdMob when the app makes network requests. AdMob may derive an approximate (coarse) location, such as country or city, from your IP address. Used for basic service operation and regional ad delivery.

App Usage Data
Firebase collects anonymized usage data (e.g., which screens are visited, crash reports) to help us maintain and improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

Device and Ad Interaction Data
When ads are shown, Google AdMob receives device and app information (e.g., device model, operating system version, app version, language) and ad interaction data (e.g., ad impressions and taps).

Product Analytics (PostHog)
We use PostHog (US cloud) to understand how the app is used. It records product usage events such as screen views, feature use, and steps in the purchase flow. These events are linked to the anonymous Firebase user ID the app creates for you. Your advertising identifier is not sent to analytics, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other identity information through analytics.

Purchase Information
If you purchase a premium subscription, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple. We receive only your subscription status (active/expired) through RevenueCat. We never see or store your payment details.

Push Notification Token
If you enable watchlist notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token is generated and stored on our servers. This token is used solely to deliver notifications about content related to your watchlist. It is not shared with third parties.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email, phone number, or any account credentials
  • Precise (GPS) location. An approximate location is only derived from your IP address, as described above.
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Health or biometric data
  • Financial or payment information

5. Advertising, Consent and App Tracking Transparency

Military Atlas shows personalized ads through Google AdMob where the law and your choices allow it: on iOS only if you allow tracking, in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland only if you consent, and on Android elsewhere unless you opt out in your device settings. Ads that are not personalized are still selected using limited information such as approximate location and the app's content, and Google may use data for ad measurement and fraud prevention.

iOS: App Tracking Transparency

On iOS 14.5 and later, we request permission to use your device's advertising identifier under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. A system prompt will appear asking:

"Military Atlas would like permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies."
  • If you allow tracking: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is shared with Google and its advertising partners, and ads may be personalized.
  • If you decline tracking: you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

You can change this preference at any time in:
iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Military Atlas

Android

Outside the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads on Android may be personalized using your Android advertising ID without a separate prompt in the app. You can opt out of ad personalization or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path may vary by device).

EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will see Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, based on the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). If you do not give consent, only non-personalized or limited ads are shown.

Changing Your Choice

In the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can review or change your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. On iOS you can change tracking under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; on Android, under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Pro and Founder users do not see ads and are not asked for these permissions.

10. Account Deletion & Data Removal

Military Atlas automatically creates an anonymous account when you first open the app. No email address or personal identity is required or collected.

You have the right to delete all data associated with your account at any time.

How to delete your data

  1. Open the app and go to Settings
  2. Scroll to the Account section
  3. Tap Delete My Data
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog

This action permanently deletes: your watchlist, your push notification token, your language and timezone preferences, and your anonymous Firebase account record.

What is not affected: Active subscriptions are managed exclusively by Apple. To cancel, go to iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions.

Data deletion is permanent and irreversible.

2. 我们收集的信息

2.1 您提供的信息

我们不要求创建账户。我们不收集您的姓名、电子邮件地址、电话号码或任何其他个人身份信息。

您的关注列表（保存的国家和装备）以匿名方式存储在我们的服务器上，仅与随机生成的设备标识符关联。

2.2 自动收集的信息

设备广告标识符（IDFA / Advertising ID）
在允许的情况下（见第5节），Google AdMob 会读取您设备的广告标识符，并与 Google 及其广告合作伙伴共享，以便对广告进行个性化和效果衡量。在 iOS 上需要您的许可；在欧洲经济区、英国和瑞士需要您的同意；在其他地区的 Android 设备上，您可以在设备设置中选择退出。否则，广告将以非个性化方式展示。该标识符由您的操作系统分配，可重置且非永久。Pro 和 Founder 用户不会看到广告，也不会被请求此权限。

IP地址和大致位置
在应用发出网络请求时，由我们的云基础设施（Firebase/Google）和 Google AdMob 自动收集。AdMob 可能根据您的 IP 地址推断大致位置（如国家或城市）。用于基本服务运行和区域广告投放。

应用使用数据
Firebase收集匿名使用数据（如访问的页面、崩溃报告）。这些数据与您的身份无关。

设备信息和广告互动数据

购买信息
高级订阅完全由Apple处理。我们仅通过RevenueCat接收您的订阅状态。

推送通知令牌
若您启用关注列表通知，将生成FCM令牌并存储在我们的服务器上，仅用于发送通知。
